# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, sexual
orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/beremaran). Reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors for behavior considered inappropriate or harmful.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
